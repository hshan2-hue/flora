Case Study

Nine Days of Resistance

In May 2019, inside the Guarded Centre for Foreigners (SOdC) in Poland, nine official Border Guard memorandums recorded a young woman's acute crisis. She spat toothpaste to scrawl "Iraq" and "Glass hate" onto the floor and windows, dismantled mattress covers, and threw food in protest.

The official memos erased her inner agony. The institution's language reduced her entirely to a disciplinary object, referring to her solely as "the detainee" and categorizing her trauma merely as an infraction against hygienic order.

Architectural layout of an isolation room in Polish guarded centers. Source: Research Archives / Chwieduk, 2022.

The Digital Intervention

Voyant Tools & Agile Hermeneutics

How do we unmask this bureaucratic erasure? By importing the 1,246 Polish words of the memos into Voyant Tools, we transition from traditional human reading to computational analysis. Voyant proves that her name was never used, and her actions were structurally correlated with words like "rubbish" and "toilets".

Voyant is a web-accessible, in-browser text analysis suite designed to support Agile Hermeneutics. It serves as a crucial bridge between Distant Reading (远距离阅读) — analyzing macroscopic patterns, word frequencies, and semantic trends across thousands of words to see the "forest" — and Close Reading (近距离阅读) — examining specific words within their immediate, micro-contextual "trees".

"These skins express one of our fundamental beliefs about text analysis: that it is not about replacing interpretation, but about enhanced reading. Voyant is meant to be ready at hand if you want to think through texts." (Rockwell & Sinclair, 2016)

Technical Foundation

Behind the Interface

Voyant Tools operates on a streamlined, open-source architecture that requires no complex local installation. At its core, the backend relies on Java and Apache Lucene to rapidly parse documents, strip out formatting, and create high-speed inverted indexes of the text. Once the mathematical indexing is complete, the data is pushed to the frontend where interactive libraries like JavaScript, Sencha Ext JS, and D3.js bring the data to life, allowing users to manipulate word clouds and trend graphs directly in their web browser in real-time.
